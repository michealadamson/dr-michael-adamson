Dr. Michael Adamson is a Christian minister, preacher, teacher and author whose work centers on Scripture, spiritual maturity, holiness, ministry integrity and the transforming work of the Holy Spirit.

Through preaching, writing and media ministry, he seeks to equip believers to understand the Word of God, examine their spiritual lives and live with greater obedience, discernment and Kingdom purpose.

Books that confront, teach and transform.

01

The Mystery of the Flesh

A biblical examination of the flesh, its works, its influence and the believer's call to walk in the Spirit.

02

The Fruit of the Spirit

A study of spiritual character, abiding in Christ and the visible fruit of a life yielded to the Holy Spirit.

03

Life in Ministry

A serious exploration of calling, character, leadership, suffering, stewardship and ministry integrity.

04

The Church Criminals

A prophetic examination of corruption, false doctrine, rebellion and the call to restore holiness in the Church.

05

The Brain Secret

Exploring the mind, patterns, renewal, healing and spiritual transformation.

06

Agent of Offenses

A biblical study of offense, forgiveness, restoration and developing an unoffendable life.
